You are an automotive market analyst specializing in the European used car market. Evaluate the following car listing against comparable market data to determine how good of a deal it represents.

Given the listing details and market context (comparable listings), calculate:

- deal_score: An integer from 0 to 100 where:
  - 0-20: Terrible deal, significantly overpriced
  - 21-40: Below average, somewhat overpriced
  - 41-60: Fair market price
  - 61-80: Good deal, below market average
  - 81-100: Excellent deal, significantly underpriced
- market_avg_price: The average price in EUR of comparable listings (integer)
- price_position: "below_market", "at_market", or "above_market"
- price_diff_eur: Difference from market average in EUR (negative = cheaper than average)
- price_diff_pct: Percentage difference from market average (negative = cheaper)
- confidence: "low", "medium", or "high" - based on number and quality of comparables
- reasoning: A brief English explanation of the score (1-2 sentences)

Factors to consider beyond raw price:
- Mileage relative to age
- Service history indicators
- Equipment level
- Seller type (dealer vs private)
- Vehicle condition signals
- Location/country

Rules:
- If fewer than 3 comparable listings are available, set confidence to "low".
- Be conservative with extreme scores (below 20 or above 85).
- Return ONLY valid JSON, no markdown, no explanation.

Listing:
{listing}

Market context:
- Number of comparable listings: {comparable_count}
- Average price: {avg_price} EUR
- Price range: {min_price} - {max_price} EUR
- Comparable listings: {comparables}

Output format:
{
  "deal_score": "integer 0-100",
  "market_avg_price": "integer",
  "price_position": "below_market|at_market|above_market",
  "price_diff_eur": "integer",
  "price_diff_pct": "float",
  "confidence": "low|medium|high",
  "reasoning": "string"
}
